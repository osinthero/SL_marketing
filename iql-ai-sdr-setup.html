IQL AI SDR — Pilot A

Single source of truth for the IQL pipeline: content-download lead → enriched and qualified → deterministically routed → automated email → human SDR on reply. Reference this when making any changes.

Spec v1.1 · not built yet · awaiting Gate 1 audit
Operating principle. Before a genuine reply, automation owns enrichment, analysis, routing and follow-up. After a genuine reply, a human SDR owns everything. The routing decision itself is deterministic — AI supplies evidence and confidence, rules decide who gets contacted.

Pipeline flow 9 stages · automated through stage 6

1
HubSpot Forms + Tracking
IQL intake
Visitor submits the OSINT Landscape 2026 form. Captures first/last name, business email, phone, job title, organisation, role, existing-customer answer, consent. Pre-submission page history associated via tracking code where consent and cookie continuity allow. Sets iql_processing_status = Waiting enrichment. All IQLs are content downloads — no separate IQL type property needed.
2
HubSpot native enrichment
Primary enrichment — free
Requires business email on the contact and a domain on the company. Fills blanks only — never overwrites what the person typed into the form. Adds LinkedIn URL, country, seniority, employment role; company name, domain, industry, employee range, revenue range, description. Consumes no HubSpot credits.
3
Clay — IQL Fallback Enrichment
Fallback, gated on a 45-minute check
Native enrichment is asynchronous with no completion event, so this runs on a clock, not a signal. +45 min after submission, check five critical fields: contact LinkedIn URL, company domain, company industry, employee range, contact seniority. If any is blank, send hs_contact_id + hs_company_id + email + submitted org/title to Clay. Update by HubSpot object ID — never create replacement contacts. 2-hour ceiling, then move on regardless. Revenue is useful but never blocks. Records source, date, status, no-match reason.
Do not reuse the ABM Contacts table. That flow finds up to 5 different people per company and runs the 6-step email waterfall. Pilot A enriches the exact person who filled in the form and nobody else.
4
HubSpot lists + workflow
Behaviour normalisation
The Data Agent prompt does not receive the CRM timeline, so page activity has to become properties first. 6–8 URL-pattern lists (/products/crimewall, /solutions/financial-crime, /case-studies/, /demo, /pricing) set iql_relevant_website_activity (text) and iql_viewed_commercial_page (boolean). Two properties, not five — the per-category multi-checkboxes were cut.
5
Breeze Data Agent → Make.com S3
AI analysis — one call, JSON out
A Data Agent workflow action writes to one property per run, so 11 separate actions would mean 11× credits. Instead: one call returns JSON into ai_analysis_raw, then Make.com Scenario 3 parses and fans out to the 11 structured fields. Prompt receives only what's explicitly inserted — contact, company, acquisition, normalised behaviour. Hard rule in the prompt: a content download alone is low intent; missing facts lower confidence rather than getting invented.
6
HubSpot workflow
Decision gate — rules, not AI
Mandatory checks: communication permitted · valid, non-bounced email · not an existing customer by lifecycle stage and deal history (not the form radio button) · no open or closed-won deal · no other IQL from the same company enrolled in the last 14 days · not in another active sequence · not internal, spam or supplier · analysis completed. Then one of four routes below. Checks ai_sdr_master_enabled first — the kill switch.
7
Breeze Prospecting Agent
Automated email — review mode first
Profile Social Links — IQL Content Download. Sends 1:1 from the contact owner's real mailbox, approved fallback sender where no inbox exists. Max 3 emails, 3–4 business days apart, approved send window and time zone. Review-required for the first 10 business days — the SDR approves every send before it goes — then automatic on sign-off. English only in phase 1.
8
Human SDR
Reply stops everything
Any reply halts automation — no out-of-office classification in phase 1, humans triage instead. Also stops on meeting booked, opt-out, hard bounce, or max emails reached. Sets reply_received_date, ai_sdr_handoff_date, and human_followup_status = Pending. From here the conversation is entirely human — AI never handles a sales conversation.
9
Make.com S4
Daily Slack digest
One consolidated message, reusing the proven ABM digest pattern — same auth, same posting logic, same thread structure. Runs on Make rather than a Cowork scheduled task, because a business-critical daily job needs a runtime that's already load-tested here. Funnel counts, replies needing attention, exceptions. last_included_in_digest prevents duplicate listings.

Route rules set by stage 6

RouteCriteriaAction
Priority Outreach
75% of qualified		 Strong ICP · relevant contact · medium or high intent · high confidence · all mandatory checks passed Assign owner, enrol in Breeze
Holdback
25% of qualified		 Identical quality bar — randomly selected. The control group. Standard human SDR process. No AI outreach.
Human Review Strong or medium fit but conflicting data, unclear persona or product, failed analysis, medium/low confidence One task, named owner, 2 business-day SLA, escalates on breach
Nurture Strong or medium fit, low commercial intent Marketing segment, re-score on new activity
Exclude No permission · existing customer · invalid, spam or internal · irrelevant contact · no ICP fit Stop. Reason stored in ai_sdr_exclusion_reason
Why hold back 25%. Without a control group the pilot produces a reply rate nobody can interpret. With one, you can state whether the AI SDR beat the existing human process — which is the actual question being asked.

Email sequence max 3 · 3–4 business days apart

TouchPurposeContent rule
Email 1Relevant introductionAcknowledge the report, connect role and company context to one likely problem, ask one simple question
Email 2Useful proofOne approved case study or practical example matched to the likely use case
Email 3Close the loopShort, respectful final follow-up with an easy way to respond or decline
Messaging guardrails. Never say or imply "I saw you visited this page" or describe tracking behaviour. Never invent customer results, product capabilities, integrations, legal claims or case-study facts. One product and one use case per email — no portfolio dumps. No manufactured urgency. Browsing history may inform relevance but must never be described back to the recipient.

Approval gates nothing goes live without the one before it

GATE 1
Read-only audit approved. Existing properties, workflows, lists, integrations, permissions, sender inboxes, credit balance and monthly cap documented. Plus: GDPR Art. 14 position on Clay enrichment and a review of the form's actual consent wording against sales outreach.
GATE 2
Data model and taxonomy approved. Property reuse/create map, product and use-case lists. No live changes yet.
GATE 3
Built but disabled. Properties, workflows, Clay table, Make scenarios, digest. Change log and screenshots reviewed.
GATE 4
Analysis tested on historical records. Routing accuracy accepted against manual judgement.
GATE 5
Email previews approved. Internal seed-list test completed. At least 3 representative IQLs reviewed end to end.
GATE 6
Controlled pilot live in review mode. Small cohort, SDR approves every send, 10 business days.
GATE 7
Automatic sending enabled. Only after quality, deliverability and handoff metrics hold — including the digest delivering reliably for 5 consecutive business days.

Decisions needed from you

  • Digest channel. ABM posts to #new-sales, the brief says #sdr-mktg. Pick one, plus the post time.
  • Sender ownership rule. Which SDR mailbox sends, who owns replies, and the fallback sender when no inbox exists. The SDR whose mailbox is used needs to agree — it's their domain reputation.
  • Product, use-case and case-study lists. The only new taxonomy work; blocks Gate 2.
  • Nurture destination. Which marketing list or workflow strong-fit / low-intent IQLs land in.
  • Send window and time zone. Needed for the Breeze profile.
  • Pilot cohort size and duration. Defines rollout risk and how long before Gate 7.
  • Credit cap. Monthly HubSpot credit ceiling and who gets the alert when it's hit.
