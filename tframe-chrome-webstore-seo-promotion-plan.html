Before publishing
Step 1 2–3 days · cannot be undone later Choose the name

Only ~35 characters show in search results. A keyword here counts more than the same keyword anywhere else. Renaming after launch means another review and loses recognition, so decide now.

  • Type our tier-1 keywords into Chrome Web Store search and note what it suggests. Also try "scam a", "scam b", "scam c" — each letter gives different suggestions. Use a clean browser profile.
  • Check whether "scam" or "fraud" gets more suggestions. This decides between the options below.
  • Pick one of the three options. Get brand sign-off.

Step 4 1 hour Set the category and fill every field

Empty fields make a listing look abandoned. For a security tool that costs installs.

  • Category: Privacy & Security. Google's own definition of this category is "VPNs, password safes, and phishing deterrence". Exact match, and it has under 4,000 listings versus 86,000+ in Tools.
  • Fill homepage URL, support URL and support email. All three.
  • Set the language to English, and add the locales we plan to translate later.
  • Request the narrowest permissions that work. Permissions are shown at install and affect whether people finish installing.
  • Make the Privacy tab, the limited-use certification and the privacy policy match each other exactly. Mismatches here are a common rejection reason.

Lead timeProduct
Step 6 Day 0 · one 48-hour window Push all first installs at once

This is the step that makes the keywords work. Install count is a ranking input, and speed matters as much as the total. 3,000 installs in two days ranks better than 3,000 spread over two months. Perfect text on a listing with 40 users loses to average text on one with 400,000.

  • Email the whole early-access list in one send. Not in batches.
  • Roll out to employees at pilot accounts in the same window.
  • Link straight to the store listing, not to a landing page. Every extra click loses installs.
  • Post on Trusterity's LinkedIn and any owned channels the same day.
Biggest impactMarketing
Step 7 Week 1–6 Get reviews fast

Rating is a ranking input and our text cannot change it. Reviews also decide whether people install after landing on the page.

  • Ask for a review inside the product, right after a low Trust Score. That is the moment we just saved someone. Never ask on install, and never after a clean result.
  • Reply to every review, including the bad ones. A well-handled 1-star builds more trust than an ignored 5-star.
  • Never offer anything in exchange for a review. That is a ban-level violation, not a warning.

After launch
Step 8 Weekly, from week 1 Track which searches show us

Google gives no ranking data for the store. Manual checking is the only way to know if this is working.

  • Search each tier-1 and tier-2 keyword in a clean browser profile. Record our position.
  • Keep it in one sheet, dated. One row per keyword per week.
  • Track installs and rating from the developer dashboard in the same sheet.
Weekly · 30 minMarketing
Step 9 Weeks 2–6 Improve the descriptions using that data

The name is locked. Both descriptions are not. This is where the remaining upside is.

  • Swap in keywords we are not ranking for. Drop ones that show nothing after four weeks.
  • Change one field at a time. Wait two weeks between changes.
  • Write the date of every edit in the tracking sheet. Two changes at once tells us nothing.
OngoingMarketing
Step 10 Month 2–3 Translate the listing

Each language is its own search index with far less competition. Do it after the English version shows which words work.

  • Get the list of regions where Trusterity sells. That sets the order. Likely German, Spanish, French first. Not Brazil or Indonesia unless we sell there.
  • Run store autocomplete inside each language before writing. A translator gives correct words. Only autocomplete shows which correct word people type. Example: "golpe" not "fraude" in Brazil, "Fake-Shop" in Germany.
  • Translate the detailed description and screenshots. Keep the feature list identical. Changing what the product does per language gets flagged automatically.
